**In the United States, GTA 6 unlocks at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, according to the game's PlayStation Store listing. Because the US unlocks all at once, players on the West Coast can start at 9:00 p.m. on Wednesday, November 18.

## Unlock time by US time zone

| Time zone | Examples | You can play from |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | New York, Florida, Georgia, the Carolinas | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, Nov 19 |
| Central (CT) | Texas, Illinois, Louisiana | 11:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Mountain (MT) | Colorado, Utah, New Mexico | 10:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Arizona (MST) | Phoenix, Tucson | 10:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Pacific (PT) | California, Nevada, Washington | 9:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Alaska (AKT) | Anchorage | 8:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Hawaii (HST) | Honolulu | 7:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |

*Eastern Time from the PlayStation Store listing; the rest converted from it. Daylight saving time ends on November 1, so on launch day Arizona matches Mountain Time.*

**Want to be sure?** Sign in and open the game's page on the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store. The exact unlock time for your account's region is shown there.

## Is it a worldwide simultaneous launch?

No. GTA 6 unlocks at **midnight local time in each region**, starting in Oceania and moving west. According to Hipertextual, there are about 19 hours between the first and the last players to get in. In Spain, for example, it unlocks at midnight local time, six hours before the US East Coast. Rockstar has not published an official country-by-country schedule; our Spanish guide keeps [the times for Spain and Latin America](/guias/gta-6-a-que-hora-sale/).

## When does the preload start?

If you buy or pre-order the **digital** version, you can **preload from November 12, 2026**, a week before launch. That way, when your unlock time arrives, you only need to open the game. Make room first: it's expected to be a big download — see our [GTA 6 file size guide](/en/gta-6-file-size/).

## What if I buy the boxed version?

The physical edition **doesn't include a disc, just a download code**. Stores may ship it a few days early, but you still can't play before the unlock time, and you'll have to download the whole game. Redeem the code as soon as you get it so the download can start.

## How to be ready at unlock time

1. **Pre-order digitally before November 12** if you want to preload.
2. **If you bought the box, redeem the code right away.**
3. **Clear plenty of storage space** — around 200 GB to be safe.
4. **Leave your console in rest mode with internet on** the night of the 18th, so any day-one patch downloads by itself.

## FAQ

**Is it the same time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S?** That's what's expected: midnight Eastern in the US. The time comes from the PlayStation Store; Microsoft hasn't listed a different one.

**Can I play earlier by switching my console region?** Some outlets suggest it might work on Xbox, as it has with other launches. We don't recommend it: Rockstar hasn't said so, it can cause store and account problems, and the difference is only a few hours.

**How much does it cost?** $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, plus sales tax. Details in our [price and editions guide](/en/gta-6-price-editions/).
