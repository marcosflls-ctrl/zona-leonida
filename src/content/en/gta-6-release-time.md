**In the United States, GTA 6 unlocks at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday, November 19, 2026**, Rockstar confirmed on November 3 that the game unlocks at **midnight local time on November 19 in all regions**, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Because the US unlocks all at once, players on the West Coast can start at 9:00 p.m. on Wednesday, November 18.

## Unlock time by US time zone

| Time zone | Examples | You can play from |
|---|---|---|
| Eastern (ET) | New York, Florida, Georgia, the Carolinas | 12:00 a.m., Thursday, Nov 19 |
| Central (CT) | Texas, Illinois, Louisiana | 11:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Mountain (MT) | Colorado, Utah, New Mexico | 10:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Arizona (MST) | Phoenix, Tucson | 10:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Pacific (PT) | California, Nevada, Washington | 9:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Alaska (AKT) | Anchorage | 8:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |
| Hawaii (HST) | Honolulu | 7:00 p.m., Wednesday, Nov 18 |

*Eastern Time per Rockstar and the PlayStation Store listing; the rest converted from it. Daylight saving time ends on November 1, so on launch day Arizona matches Mountain Time.*

**Want to be sure?** Sign in and open the game's page on the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store. The exact unlock time for your account's region is shown there.

## Is it a worldwide simultaneous launch?

No. GTA 6 unlocks at **midnight local time in each region**, starting in Oceania and moving west. According to Hipertextual, there are about 19 hours between the first and the last players to get in. In Spain, for example, it unlocks at midnight local time, six hours before the US East Coast. Our Spanish guide keeps [the times for Spain and Latin America](/guias/gta-6-a-que-hora-sale/).

## When does the preload start?

**The preload is already open.** On PS5 it started at midnight local time on November 10, 2026; on Xbox Series X|S at 12 a.m. GMT on November 10 (7 p.m. ET on November 9). Rockstar asks for **up to 212 GB** of free space — see our [GTA 6 file size guide](/en/gta-6-file-size/).

## What if I buy the boxed version?

The physical edition **doesn't include a disc, just a download code**. Boxed copies should reach stores on November 12, but you still can't play before the unlock time, and you'll have to download the whole game. Redeem the code as soon as you get it and the preload starts right away.

## How to be ready at unlock time

1. **Pre-order digitally** if you want to preload — it's open now.
2. **If you bought the box, redeem the code right away.**
3. **Clear plenty of storage space** — Rockstar asks for up to 212 GB.
4. **Leave your console in rest mode with internet on** the night of the 18th, so any day-one patch downloads by itself.

## FAQ

**Is it the same time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S?** Yes. Rockstar confirms midnight local time on both. Only the preload started at different times.

**Can I play earlier by switching my console region?** Some outlets suggest it might work on Xbox, as it has with other launches. We don't recommend it: Rockstar hasn't said so, it can cause store and account problems, and the difference is only a few hours.

**How much does it cost?** $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, plus sales tax. Details in our [price and editions guide](/en/gta-6-price-editions/).
