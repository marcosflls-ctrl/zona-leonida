**GTA 6 is out now.** It unlocked across the United States at **12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on Thursday, November 19, 2026** — 9:00 p.m. on Wednesday the 18th on the West Coast — on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar unlocked it at **midnight local time in every region**, so New Zealand and Australia got in first and the US was among the last.

**Just bought it?** It's playable as soon as the download finishes. The full game needs up to 212 GB, plus two updates (1.002.000 and 1.002.001) — see the [GTA 6 file size guide](/en/gta-6-file-size/).

## Can I play it on Xbox Cloud Gaming or Xbox One?

**Yes, through the cloud.** Microsoft opened GTA 6 on **Xbox Cloud Gaming at 1:00 a.m. ET on November 19**, at the same time worldwide. That's the way to play it on **Xbox One**, which has no version of its own, or on a phone, tablet or PC with a controller. You still have to buy the game, and in the cloud it runs with Xbox Series S settings.

## Was it a worldwide simultaneous launch?

No. GTA 6 unlocked at **midnight local time in each region**, starting in Oceania and moving west. According to Hipertextual, there are about 19 hours between the first and the last players to get in. In Spain, for example, it unlocked at midnight local time, six hours before the US East Coast. Our Spanish guide keeps [the times for Spain and Latin America](/guias/gta-6-a-que-hora-sale/).

## What to do first

1. **Let both updates install** before you start. On PS5 the second one is about 602 MB; on Xbox, about 1.6 GB.
2. **If you bought the box, redeem the code**: it has no disc, so you'll download the whole game.
3. **PSN or Xbox Live busy?** On launch night, PlayStation Network went down for about 20 minutes. If your license doesn't show, set your console as the primary one and try again.

## FAQ

**Was it the same time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S?** Yes, midnight local time on both. Only the preload started at different times.

**How much does it cost?** $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, plus sales tax. Details in our [price and editions guide](/en/gta-6-price-editions/).
