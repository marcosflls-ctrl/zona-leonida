**GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, and the game launches on **November 19, 2026**. At $79.99, the Standard Edition is $10 more than the usual price of a big console release.

## Editions at a glance

| Edition | US price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $79.99 | The full game |
| Ultimate | $99.99 | The game plus extra vehicles, weapons, businesses, clothing and more, spread across the story |
| Upgrade from Standard to Ultimate | No official price yet | The Ultimate extras |

Prices are before **sales tax**, which depends on your state and city — the digital stores also charge it based on your billing address.

## What does the Ultimate Edition add?

Rockstar describes it as a set of exclusive items spread throughout Jason and Lucia's story. According to the official details reported by Gematsu, it includes:

- **Vehicles:** the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, the Shitzu Squalo speedboat and a retro kit for Jason's Vapid Ganado.
- **Two exclusive garages** (Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's) and a side job restoring classic cars.
- **Weapons**, such as the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers.
- **Exclusive shops, clothing, hairstyles and a tattoo parlor.**

None of it is required to finish the game. If you're unsure, you can buy the Standard Edition and upgrade later: an upgrade already appears on the PlayStation Store, though without a price.

## Pre-order bonuses

There are two bonuses for pre-ordering or buying **before November 20, 2026**:

- **Vintage Vice City Pack** (any edition, physical or digital): a two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier, a garage in Shore Court near Ocean Beach, and retro outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia.
- **One month of GTA+** (digital pre-orders): Rockstar's subscription service. It **renews automatically** unless you cancel, and must be redeemed by March 31, 2027.

## Is the physical edition worth it?

The box **contains a download code, not a disc**. It makes sense if you want the box as a keepsake or find a store deal, but you'll download the whole game either way. Major US retailers such as GameStop, Best Buy, Walmart, Target and Amazon carry it — compare prices and check whether the pre-order bonus is included.

## The Vice City collector's box

Rockstar also sells **The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection**, a collector's box of Vice City merchandise (€399.99 in Europe; check Rockstar's store for the US price). **It doesn't include the game.**

## FAQ

**Is GTA 6 on PS4, Xbox One or PC?** No. It's out on PS5 (including PS5 Pro) and Xbox Series X|S only. A PC version hasn't been announced.

**Is there online multiplayer at launch?** Rockstar presents GTA 6 as a single-player experience and hasn't detailed an online mode for launch.
