**Rockstar hasn't confirmed how big GTA 6 is yet.** Early estimates were **100–150 GB**; more recent ones go up to **150–200 GB**. To be safe, keep **about 200 GB free**. The official size usually appears on the store listing a few days before the preload, which starts on **November 12, 2026**. We'll update this page as soon as it does.

## Size estimates

| Scenario | Estimated size |
|---|---|
| Base install | 100–130 GB |
| With all launch content | 150–200 GB |

These are analyst estimates reported by outlets such as Infobae, not official figures. Some October estimates put the base install at 150–200 GB, and up to 200–250 GB with a day-one patch — also unconfirmed. For reference, GTA 5 takes about 87 GB on PS5, and Red Dead Redemption 2 is over 100 GB.

## How much space does your console have?

| Console | Total storage | Approx. usable space |
|---|---|---|
| PS5 (original) | 825 GB | ~667 GB |
| PS5 Slim / PS5 Pro | 1 TB / 2 TB | ~848 GB / ~1.8 TB |
| Xbox Series X | 1 TB | ~802 GB |
| Xbox Series S | 512 GB | ~364 GB |

**Xbox Series S owners will feel it most:** GTA 6 could take up almost half of its usable space. Clear room before November 12.

## Can I play GTA 6 from a USB hard drive?

**No.** A USB drive can store games, but current-gen games can't be played from it. Sony says it plainly: PS5 games can't be played directly from USB extended storage. On Xbox Series X|S, games optimized for the console must run from internal storage or the official expansion card.

What you *can* do is **move other games to the USB drive** to free up internal space for GTA 6, then move them back later without downloading them again.

## How to check your free space

- **PS5:** Settings → Storage → Console Storage.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** Settings → System → Storage devices. You can also sort "My games & apps" by size to see what to delete first.

## Getting your console ready

- **Clean up before November 12.** You can always re-download games you delete.
- **Check saved screenshots and video clips** — they take more space than you'd think.
- **Running tight? Consider expanding storage:** a compatible M.2 SSD on PS5, or an official expansion card on Xbox Series.
- **Leave extra headroom.** During installation and patching, the game can temporarily need more space than its final size.
