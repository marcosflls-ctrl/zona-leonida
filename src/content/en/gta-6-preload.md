**The GTA 6 preload has been open since Tuesday, November 10, 2026**, nine days before launch. You can preload if you pre-ordered digitally or if you have the **boxed copy**, which comes with a download code instead of a disc. You need **up to 212 GB** of free space.

| Console | When the preload opened |
|---|---|
| PS5 | Midnight local time on November 10 |
| Xbox Series X\|S | 12 a.m. GMT on November 10 — that's 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT on Monday, November 9 |

The game itself unlocks on **November 19**. Check [your exact unlock time](/en/gta-6-release-time/).

## How to preload GTA 6 on PS5

1. Make sure you have **at least 212 GB free**: *Settings → Storage*. Not enough? See the [file size guide](/en/gta-6-file-size/) for what to delete or move.
2. Open the **PlayStation Store** and go to the GTA 6 page, or find the game in your **Game Library** under *Purchased*.
3. Select **Download**.
4. Leave the console on or in **rest mode** with *Stay Connected to the Internet* enabled, so the download keeps going.

If you turned on automatic downloads for pre-orders, the console starts on its own.

## How to preload GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S

1. Check your free space: *Settings → System → Storage devices*. On a **Series S** (about 364 GB usable), GTA 6 takes more than half the drive.
2. Go to **My games & apps → Full library → All owned games** and find GTA 6, or open its page in the **Microsoft Store**.
3. Select **Install**.
4. Keep the console in **Sleep** mode rather than Energy saving, so it downloads while off.

## I bought the boxed copy. Can I preload?

Yes. The box contains a **code, not a disc**. Boxed copies reach stores on **November 12**. Redeem the code in the PlayStation Store or the Microsoft Store and the preload starts right away. The full download still applies, so the box doesn't save you any space or time.

## How long will the download take?

It depends on your connection. With 212 GB, as a rough guide:

| Your speed | Approximate time |
|---|---|
| 100 Mbps | About 4 h 45 min |
| 300 Mbps | About 1 h 35 min |
| 500 Mbps | About 1 hour |
| 1 Gbps | About 30 min |

These are best-case figures. Real speeds are usually lower than the plan's, especially over Wi-Fi, and servers are busier in launch week. Use a wired connection if you can.

## The preload won't start. What can I do?

- **Check your storage first.** If you don't have 212 GB free, the console won't start the download.
- **Check you're on the account that owns the game** and that the console is online.
- **Restart the console** and try again from the store page.
- **Boxed copy?** The download only appears after you redeem the code.
- **Still stuck?** Check the PlayStation Network or Xbox status page: on busy days the stores can be slow.

## Can I play as soon as it's downloaded?

No. The preload only downloads the files. The game unlocks at your region's launch time on November 19 — in the US, that's **12 a.m. ET on Thursday, November 19**, which is 9 p.m. PT on Wednesday the 18th. Full table in our [release time guide](/en/gta-6-release-time/).
