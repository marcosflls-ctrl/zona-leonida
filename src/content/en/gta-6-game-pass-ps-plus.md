**No. GTA 6 is not on Xbox Game Pass or PlayStation Plus at launch.** Neither Rockstar, Microsoft nor Sony has announced it, and Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, has said for years that its biggest releases don't go into subscriptions on day one. To play on November 19, 2026, you have to **buy the game**.

## Why isn't it in a subscription?

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick put it plainly on a 2023 earnings call, as reported by PlayStation LifeStyle: the company does not put its frontline titles into subscription services on the day they launch.

The reason is money. GTA 6 is the most anticipated game in years, and Take-Two expects to sell it at full price to millions of players. Including it in a subscription on day one would cut into those sales.

## What about Xbox Cloud Gaming? It's not the same thing

This is where most of the confusion comes from. According to Infobae, Microsoft has the rights to offer GTA 6 on **Xbox Cloud Gaming** from launch, but that **doesn't mean it's included with Game Pass**:

- The cloud lets you stream a game **you already own** instead of downloading it.
- Xbox has clarified that GTA 6 **won't be playable on PC** through the cloud. Infobae adds that phones and browsers are excluded too.
- Since November, Game Pass cloud play has a **monthly hour limit**: 15 hours on Ultimate, 10 on Premium and 5 on Essential, according to the same report.

## Will it come to Game Pass or PS Plus later?

Possibly, but not soon. The precedent is **GTA 5**: it launched in 2013 and, according to VGC, joined Xbox Game Pass in January 2020 — more than six years later. GTA 6 will sell at full price for years, so expect a similar wait or longer.

## What do you need a subscription for?

- **For the story: nothing.** You can play it without PS Plus or Game Pass.
- **For online play**, if and when it arrives, you'd normally need PS Plus or Game Pass, as with every online console game. Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6's online mode yet.
- **GTA+** is Rockstar's own subscription. Digital pre-orders include one free month, which **renews automatically** unless you cancel. More in our [price and editions guide](/en/gta-6-price-editions/).
